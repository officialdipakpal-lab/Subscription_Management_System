🎬 5-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

Follow the exact scenes from the problem statement to demonstrate every requirement live to judges.

1

Scene 1: Customer Signup, Password Policy & Forgot Password

Client and server enforced: > 8 chars, 1 uppercase, 1 lowercase, 1 special character. Single-use time-limited reset tokens.

2

Scene 2: Product with Odoo Brand Variant (+560 extra price)

Configured in catalog: BrightERP Suite base price ₹1,200 + Odoo Brand ₹560 = ₹1,760.

3

Scene 3: Turn Quote Template into Subscription & Activate

Prefills plan, lines, taxes, and validity period; tracks the pipeline Draft → Quotation → Confirmed → Active.

4

Scene 4: Billing Day & Exactly-Once Idempotency Guarantee

Batch bills due subscriptions automatically. Running it repeatedly produces 0 duplicate invoices.

5

Scene 5: Customer Discount (15% Off with Usage Limit)

Rule ODOO15 automatically applies 15% discount on billing day and increments usage counter atomically.

6

Scene 6: Payment Recording, Balance Tracking & Settled Status

Supports partial payments across UPI, Card, Bank Wire. Automatically flips invoice from Confirmed to Paid when balance hits 0.

7

Scene 7: Month-End Finance Dashboard (< 2s Latency)

Real-time calculations for MRR, total collected, unpaid amounts, and overdue invoices with CSV export.
